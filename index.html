Who We Are

Bringing the Authentic Flavours of Kerala to the World

100% Premium Export Quality

Sneha Exim is an India-based trading company rooted in the land of spices, Kerala—a region renowned globally for its exceptional spices. We specialize in sourcing and exporting a carefully selected range of Indian spices, rice, fruits, and vegetables.

We work closely with trusted farmers, growers, and suppliers to ensure that every product meets the rigid quality expectations of our international partners and customers.
