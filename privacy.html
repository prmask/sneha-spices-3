Privacy Policy

GDPR Compliance & Data Protection Policy

Last Updated: August 13, 2026

1. Introduction

At Sneha Exim, we respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data in accordance with the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) (EU) 2016/679. This privacy policy will inform you about how we look after your personal data when you visit our website, use our inquiry forms, and tell you about your privacy rights.

2. Data We Collect

We only collect data that is voluntarily submitted by you through our wholesale inquiry form, which includes:

  • Company Name
  • Contact Person Name
  • Business Email Address
  • Country & Port of Discharge
  • VAT ID / Tax Number (optional)
  • Selected products, quantities, and custom logistics requirements

3. How We Use Your Data

We process this data for the sole purpose of responding to your B2B inquiries, generating wholesale quotes, and organizing logistics calculations. The legal basis for processing is to take steps at your request prior to entering into a contract (GDPR Article 6(1)(b)).

4. Data Sharing & Security

Your data is kept strictly confidential. We do not sell or lease your business details to third parties. We implement secure data collection and processing methods, storing details strictly for corporate communication. Some data may be shared with trusted logistics/customs agents to draft shipping calculations (e.g., freight forwarders) to fulfill your quotation request.

5. Data Retention

We will only retain your personal data for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes we collected it for, including for the purposes of satisfying any legal, accounting, or trade reporting requirements.

6. Your Rights Under GDPR

If you are located in the European Union, you have the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • Right of access: Request a copy of the data we hold on you.
  • Right to rectification: Correct any incorrect or incomplete information.
  • Right to erasure ("right to be forgotten"): Request deletion of your details from our records.
  • Right to restrict processing: Stop us from processing your data temporarily.
  • Right to data portability: Request transfer of your data to another organization.

To exercise any of these rights, please contact our European Sales & Compliance team at export@snehaexim.com.
